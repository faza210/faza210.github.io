rclone (Google Cloud app)

Used only by Emir Faza

This page covers the app called "rclone" that I registered in Google Cloud under my own Google account. It is a private tool. I use it with rclone on my own computer to copy and back up files between my own cloud storage accounts (Google Drive and Microsoft OneDrive) and my own drives.

The app is not offered to the public. It is only used with Google accounts that I manage and add to it myself.

When I sign in, the app asks Google for access to my Google Drive files so rclone can list, upload, download and delete them when I tell it to. It does not ask for access to Gmail, Contacts, Calendar or any other Google service.

Files only move between my Google Drive, my other storage accounts and my own computer. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and nothing is used for advertising, analytics or training AI models. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

The sign-in token that rclone receives is stored only on my own computer, in a file that only the administrator account can read. I can revoke it at any time from the permissions page of my Google account.
